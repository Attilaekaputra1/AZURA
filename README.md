## ☾ About Azura

Azura is a personal AI companion built for Discord, designed to bring a more personal and intelligent experience into online communities.

She goes beyond simple command-response interactions. Her development explores conversational intelligence, memory, autonomous behavior, voice interaction, web exploration, and creative tools, all brought together under one identity.

From everyday conversations to more complex interactions, Azura is an ongoing attempt to make AI feel less like a tool and more like a companion.

**One companion. Many ways to interact.**

**Azura is still in a very early stage of development.**

Although the project already contains various systems and experimental capabilities, not everything is fully implemented, stable, or working as intended.

Some features may behave inconsistently, produce unexpected results, or remain incomplete. Others may require further testing, adjustments, and refinement before they can be considered reliable.

Things will break. Things will change. And some ideas may take longer than expected to get right.

That's perfectly fine. This is part of building something from the ground up.
